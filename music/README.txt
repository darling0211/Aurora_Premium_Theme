# Music Folder

Place your background music file here as `love-theme.mp3`.

The path is configurable in `config.js` under `backgroundMusic`.

For a fully offline experience, use any royalty-free romantic instrumental track (MP3 format, ideally under 5MB for fast loading).

Recommended sources:
- Pixabay Music (free, no attribution required)
- Free Music Archive
- Bensound (free with attribution)

Just name your file `love-theme.mp3` and place it in this folder, or update the path in `config.js` to match your filename.
